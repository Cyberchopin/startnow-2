# Start Now — 3-Minute IncludAI Demo

Target runtime: 2:50–2:58. Record at 1080p, 16:9, with browser zoom near 100%. The product must remain readable after YouTube compression.

## 0:00–0:18 — The missing moment

**Screen:** Start Now homepage, no cursor movement for the first two seconds. Then hover over “School assignment.”

**Narration:**

> Most study tools assume the learner has already started. But for many neurodivergent learners, the hardest moment comes before planning: the assignment matters, the deadline is real, and the first physical action still feels impossible. Start Now is built for that activation gap.

## 0:18–0:42 — Ten-second check-in

**Screen:** Select School assignment, Low energy, Flooded overwhelm, “I'm afraid I'll do it badly,” and type “Start the machine learning homework.”

**Narration:**

> Instead of asking for another schedule, Start Now asks for ten seconds of context: available energy, overwhelm, the task, and the barrier blocking it. This learner is low-energy, flooded, and afraid of doing the work badly.

## 0:42–1:15 — Meaningful AI, constrained

**Screen:** Click “Give me one executable move.” Hold on the Gemini source badge, mission, rationale, and voice choices. Click “Still too big” once.

**Narration:**

> Gemini 3.6 reasons over that state and returns one structured, reversible action that can begin within sixty seconds—not a plan, diagnosis, or motivational lecture. The interface also explains why the action matches the barrier. If it is still too large, one click removes more scope. Invalid model output is rejected, and a deterministic safety engine keeps the intervention available during an outage.

## 1:15–1:42 — Body Double and honest action

**Screen:** Choose one voice persona, start the physical action, show the timer for several seconds, then click “I took the action.”

**Narration:**

> An optional Gemini voice Body Double stays with the learner without demanding completion. The contract is only to create one visible trace. The learner can pause or ask for an even smaller move at any time.

## 1:42–2:02 — Proof, not intention

**Screen:** Enter “I opened the homework and wrote a rough first line.” Select Lighter. Save “Continue the first answer.” Count the honest start.

**Narration:**

> Start Now does not award progress for intention. The learner names the specific action their hands took and saves the exact re-entry point. That lowers tomorrow's restart cost and creates behavior evidence without surveillance.

## 2:02–2:20 — Learning across sessions

**Screen:** Show Journeys, then Insights. Highlight average ignition, shrink depth, and local/private labeling.

**Narration:**

> Journeys preserve the edge of progress. Insights measure honest starts, time-to-action, relief, and shrink depth. Personal history stays on the device and never becomes a diagnosis.

## 2:20–2:43 — Designed with users

**Screen:** Show User Study guardrails and aggregate count, then cut to the private Research Console with raw text framed but not zoomed enough to expose unnecessary details. Show the recovery decision card and, if available in the clean demo profile, the “You came back” recovery state.

**Narration:**

> During the hackathon, three external adults tested the flow, including one adult with dyslexia. That participant told us recovery matters more than perfection: restarting after a missed day should count as progress. We made that feedback visible. After a learner returns and completes one honest start, Start Now recognizes the comeback and increments Recoveries instead of punishing a broken perfect streak. All research remains anonymous and protected.

## 2:43–2:58 — Close

**Screen:** Return to the completed-start screen, then end card with Start Now, live URL, GitHub, Gemini, and “One honest start.”

**Narration:**

> Start Now adapts the first move to the learner instead of asking the learner to adapt to another productivity system. No fake productivity. No pressure to finish. One honest start.

## Recording rules

- Keep the final export below 3:00; judges are not required to watch beyond the limit.
- Use no copyrighted music. Silence or an original low-volume texture is safer.
- Record the actual deployed product, not a design mockup.
- Do not show environment variables, passwords, browser bookmarks, notifications, or participant-identifying information.
- Use captions. Keep each caption to two lines or fewer.
- Upload as Unlisted or Public on YouTube or Vimeo, then test the link in a signed-out browser.
